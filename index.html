The Problem

Wildlife movement and high-speed roads collide

Animals cross roads searching for food, water and habitat — while vehicles move through the same corridors at highway speed. Existing efforts (camera traps, patrols, static signage) exist but work in silos and rarely reach the driver in time.

6,507
animals recorded in road-mortality studies across the Western Ghats, 1997–2023, spanning 239 species.

Every number is a life lost on a road that cuts through its home.

Our Solution

One system. Four steps. Three outcomes.

TerraSense is an AI-powered roadside intelligence system that detects and verifies wildlife movement in real time — then connects roadside poles to warn drivers and build a live conservation dataset.

01

Detect

Camera + Edge AI

Detects wildlife locally, in real time, right at the pole.

02

Verify

Detection validation

Confirms the detection across consecutive frames to cut false alerts.

03

Connect

Pole-to-pole network

Shares the alert with configured nearby poles — offline, over LoRa.

04

Warn

Amber beacon

Warns approaching drivers before they ever reach the area.

🛣️ Safer roads 🦌 Protect wildlife 📊 Smarter monitoring
